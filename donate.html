Wanting to support us as an organisation? You can become a sponsor by getting in touch with us.

Dundee Data Meetup is a community-driven initiative that relies on the support of sponsors to continue providing valuable content and networking opportunities for data professionals in Dundee. By sponsoring our meetup, you can help us create a vibrant and inclusive environment for data enthusiasts to learn, share, and connect.

If you would like to support Dundee Data Meetup, you can make a donation to help us cover the costs of organizing events, providing catering, and maintaining our community resources.

We use GoCardless to securely process all donations. When you choose a donation option below, you will be redirected to their platform to complete your contribution.
