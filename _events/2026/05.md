        We joined the Service Design Academy at Dundee Data Meetup for a hands‑on, creative workshop where we explored how data and storytelling come together to shape better experiences.

        In this session, we set aside dashboards and metrics and focused on how data feels, by building with LEGO.

        Together, we:

        - Explored the value of data in service design

        - Analysed small real‑world datasets

        - Translated insights into visual stories

        - Built a LEGO model that represents what the data reveals about user needs

      
        Our participants left with practical tools for data storytelling, a clearer sense of how to combine numbers and narratives, and most importantly, a memorable brick-building experience.
